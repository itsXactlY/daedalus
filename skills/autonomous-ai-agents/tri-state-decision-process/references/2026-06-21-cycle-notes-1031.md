## Two-Phase Dedup Walk: Tick-Summary + Sub-Find Independently

The 2h dedup window operates on the DECISION timestamp (when the decision was written), not on the discovery's creation time. The standard walk:

1. Find all `decision:rank-YYYYMMDD-*` memories created in the last 2h
2. For each decision, extract the discovery_id it references (regex on `Discovery Memory ID:\s*(\d+)`)
3. Skip those discovery_ids from the ranking pool

**Extension for tick-summary era (this cycle):** When a tick-summary discovery is already ranked as a decision, BOTH the tick-summary AND its sub-finds should be considered "covered" — even if the sub-finds are not individually ranked. The tick-summary decision's content typically lists all sub-find IDs, so the entire cluster is covered by one decision.

**Concrete example (this cycle):**
- 825492 (0842 tick, crypto-blockchain + math/OR) was fully covered by 3 decisions at 0845: 825505 (MHOT), 825508 (OR), 825509 (IS-cluster)
- 825504 (0855 tick, philosophy) was partially covered: 825517 (phil02 health AI policy) + 825535 (phil04 Anthropic walk-back) — but 4 sub-finds (825493, 825495, 825497, 825498) remain individually unranked
- 825532 (0942 tick, multi-domain) was only partially covered: 825535 (sub-find 825523 hyperreal robot heads) — 8 sub-finds remain individually unranked

**Decision rule:** Rank the tick-summary as a single decision IF at least 4 of its sub-finds are unranked AND the sub-finds span multiple distinct themes. Otherwise pick the highest-scoring individual sub-find.
