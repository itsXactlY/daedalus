The philosophy-of-mind cluster (826087/826089/826091/826093 + 826135/826136 + 826214) has 4 distinct angles:

- EMBODIMENT/MEANING (826093 Masi Qeios) ← this cycle's pick

The existing N=4-6 representative heuristic ("venue-anchor > mechanism-specificity > corpus-anchor") assumes axis-overlap (e.g., 4 chiplet papers all about GPU chiplets). When N=4 are 4 DISTINCT axes, the selection heuristic doesn't fit — any of the 4 is rank-able. The cycle picked 826093 (Masi Qeios) because it had the highest connectedness (0.80) AND the highest total score (2.30) — score-driven selection rather than venue-driven.

Refinement to the cluster-representative rule: when N siblings are **N distinct axes** (not N papers on the same axis), selection by highest total score is the right heuristic, not venue-anchor. Diagnostic: if each sibling's title claims a different angle (formal-def vs computational-mech vs ontological-tax vs embodiment), they are distinct-axes; if all titles claim a similar angle (e.g., all about GPU chiplets), they are axis-overlap siblings.
