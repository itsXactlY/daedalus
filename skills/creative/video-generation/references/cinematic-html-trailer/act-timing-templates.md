# Act Timing Templates

## 45-Second Viral Trailer (6 Acts)

```
ACT 0   0-2s     "EVERY AI FORGETS YOU"     Sub bass + shake
ACT 1   2-10.5s  PTSD Flashbacks (6×)        Heartbeat accelerating
ACT 2   10.5-22.5s Cross-agent recall        Cinematic rise → "THE MODEL REMEMBERED"
ACT 3   22.5-31s  Receipts (8×, 0.1s gaps)   Percussive hits
ACT 4   31-37s    OS Reframe                 Ethereal pads
ACT 5   37-45.5s  "STATELESS AI OVER" + Pricing  Climax → HARD CUT
```

## Flashback Tempo (Act 1)

| Content | Short text (1 line) | Medium (2 lines) | Long (3 lines, important) |
|---------|---------------------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| Duration | 1.0s | 1.3s | 1.7-2.0s |
| Gap before next | 0.1s | 0.1s | 0.1s |

## Receipt Bomb Tempo (Act 3)

Each receipt bomb should last 1.0-1.5s with 0.1s gap between. For 8 receipts in 8.5s:
- 8 × 1.0s = 8.0s content + 7 × 0.1s gaps = 8.7s → trim to 1.0s each with 0.06s gaps
- Or extend Act 3 to 10s and use 1.0s per receipt + 0.1s gaps
